# Faturamento — `/billing`

> Where: sidebar › Esteira › Faturamento (receipt icon; no counter) · also reached from the "N no Faturamento" pointer inside an open file on the Revisão, the Dashboard's robot/billed tiles and bill rows, and the global search's Contas results · Status: built (Pro decision 023; validated live 16/08/2026) · Code: `src/routes/billing/+page.svelte`, `src/lib/billing.svelte.ts`, `src/lib/ipc.ts` (`Bill`, `PhaseOnScreen`, `RecoverAnswer`), `src-tauri/src/billing.rs`, `src-tauri/src/lib.rs` (`bill_from_record`, `list_billing`, `ingest_bill`, `process_bill`, `recover_run`, `resolve_bill`, `withdraw_bill`), `src-tauri/src/fantasma.rs`

## The question

"O que aconteceu com a conta depois que eu aprovei?" (i18n `question.billing`; not drawn on the screen). Put as work: where is each bill, and what do I do about the one that stopped.

## Where it sits in the work

- **Third station of the conveyor.** A bill arrives here the moment Aprovar, on the [Revisão](03-review.md), writes its file into the Fantasma's queue folder. Approval also tries to hand the bill to the robot's work list on the way ("entregar"); when that fails (Docker down), the bill arrives undelivered and this screen offers "Entregar ao Fantasma".
- **The bill is a token — present on exactly one screen.** Approved, it leaves the Revisão entirely (only a quiet "N no Faturamento" pointer stays there) and lives here. "Retirar da fila" walks it back: it reappears on the Revisão under "A conferir". A finished run sweeps its queue file; a failed run keeps it (there is still work).
- **The robot.** The Fantasma is a robot in a Docker container driving the ERP (the Everest) over a remote desktop. This screen holds the app's **only** gesture that can make it write to the ERP ("Processar conta"). The robot's cycle ends at "Pedido criado"; the invoice itself is the ERP's own act, by another path.
- **No outbox.** Everything comes back through the run itself: phase announcements while the robot works, one outcome at the end. A run the app did not survive is recovered from the robot's own job trace ("Recuperar o desfecho").
- Bills approved before the billing record existed are adopted on every list read, arriving undelivered.
- The [Dashboard](01-dashboard.md) reads this screen's store (funnel, "Só anda com você", stalled buckets, Hoje). No bell notice is posted about bills: a run's result is visible here and on the Dashboard only.
- The [shell](../04-shell.md) carries the toasts, the status bar's Fantasma block and the Configurações doors this screen points at in words.

## Anatomy

Two bands: a top bar across the width, and a body split into a scrolling list and a fixed detail panel.

### 1. Top bar (divider under it)

- **Title** "Faturamento".
- **Counter line** under it: "{a} a processar · {b} provável(is) · {c} falha(s) · {d} concluída(s)" — always over **all** bills, ignoring the filters (definitions in § Data shown).
- At the right: the **client menu** button — "Todos os clientes" or the chosen client id, with a caret; opens a small menu listing "Todos os clientes" then every client id present among the bills (sorted), a check mark on the active one; closes on a choice or a click outside.
- The **view switch**, three segments: "Em andamento" (default) · "Concluídas" · "Todas".

### 2. List pane (flexible width, scrolls)

- **2a. Robot banner** — drawn only once the Fantasma's state has been answered (polled). A dot, a title and a sub line:

| Situation                                                                                              | Dot                                                            | Title                           | Sub line                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| a gesture of this window is in flight                                                                  | pulsing (static under reduced motion); green or amber as below | "Processando agora: {PACIENTE}" | follows the robot state, as below                                                      |
| robot "ready" or "foreign"                                                                             | green                                                          | "Robô pronto"                   | "Sessão aberta no Everest — pronto para processar."                                    |
| any other robot state (off, container up without session, starting, stopping) — also when unconfigured | amber                                                          | "Robô parado"                   | "Nada será processado até o Fantasma estar de pé — ligue em Configurações → Fantasma." |

- **2b. Failure banner** — conditional, red: the last refused read or gesture, verbatim. Drawn only when the view has rows.
- **2c. Column heads** (small uppercase): "Conta" · "Veredicto do Everest" · "Estágio no robô". **[functional]** verdict and stage are separate columns and never share a cell.
- **2d. Rows** — one card per bill, stacked with small gaps, newest record write first. A row is a button (click selects). Three cells:
  - **Conta** (flexible): patient name (truncates) + insurer chip; under it `{atendimento}` (mono) · `{client id}` · "{N} itens".
  - **Veredicto do Everest** (172 px): either the finished bill's document number as a neutral mono pill — "Pedido 420001" (or "Conferência 170001" when only a conferência exists) — or the verdict pill, or **nothing**; followed by "checado {age}" when an answer was recorded.
  - **Estágio no robô** (230 px): four thin segments (tooltip on each: "No Faturamento", "Conferência", "Encerrada", "Pedido criado") + a short stage text.
  - A 3 px colored bar on the row's leading edge: accent when "Processar" is available, amber when the bill needs investigation, red when it failed (red wins over accent). Selected row: accent-tinted border.
- **Empty view**: the shared `EmptyState` ([components](../05-components.md)) with a tray icon, "Nada em andamento" and "Aprovar uma conta na Revisão a coloca aqui — na fila do Fantasma, pronta para o robô."

### 3. Detail panel (fixed 352 px, divider on its left, scrolls) **[functional: the gestures live only here, beside the facts of the one selected bill]**

Always present. With no row to show: `EmptyState` with a left arrow and "Escolha uma conta na lista para ver o detalhe." Otherwise, top to bottom:

1. **Name** (patient) and a **meta line**: `{atendimento}` · `{client id}` · `{convênio}` · "{N} itens" · `{approval date dd/mm/aaaa}`.
2. **Verdict row**: the verdict pill (never the document number here) + "checado {age}" when checked.
3. **Notes**, each conditional:
   - Investigation (amber fill): "Duplicidade com dúvida — o Everest respondeu “provável”; confira no ERP antes de decidir."
   - Stopped mid-run (amber fill, hidden while this bill's own gesture is in flight): "Uma corrida ficou pelo meio — o desfecho não chegou; a barra está onde as fases pararam." + button "Recuperar o desfecho".
   - Abort (red fill): title with a warning icon = the worded abort reason; under it, in mono, the robot's own sentence verbatim.
   - Last refusal (red fill): "Última falha" + the refusal's text in mono, verbatim.
4. **Checklist** titled "O que já foi feito / o que resta": three steps — "Conferência", "Encerrada", "Pedido criado" — each with a filled check (done), a filled cross plus " — parou aqui" (the step a failed bill stopped at), or a dashed circle (still to do).
5. **Point-of-no-return note** (amber outline) when the bill is not finished and has reached "Encerrada": "A conferência já foi encerrada no Everest — retirar não desfaz; retomar o pedido é o caminho."
6. **Work-list footnote** when the robot's own list holds the bill as "revisar": "O Fantasma já processou esta conta e a linha dela saiu de pendente na lista de trabalho dele — está em revisar. Processar de novo exige devolvê-la à lista lá."
7. **Lots block** when the outcome carried per-lot answers: "O que o Everest respondeu, por lote" + one line per lot: `{lote}` (mono) — `{the Everest's phrase, verbatim}`.
8. **Actions stack** (§ Actions): the delivery button or the "Entregue ao Fantasma {age}" line (lock icon), then "Processar conta", "Assinar como resolvida", "Retirar da fila" as each applies. A confirmation opens **inline, in place of its button**, inside this stack.

## Business rules

1. **Two dimensions, never merged.** The verdict (what the Everest answered) and the stage (how far the robot took it) are different facts in different cells (023 § 4; telas.md).
2. **"Não checado" draws no pill.** It is the normal state of every bill in the queue; green exists only after someone asked. This app once queued eleven bills behind a hard-coded green "sem registro", all already invoiced (inherited rule; AGENTS.md).
3. **A finished bill wears its document number in the list**, not a pill — the fact that settles it (023 § 4).
4. **Rule 1 refusing is an ending, never a failure.** "Already invoiced" is the answer the check exists to get: nothing written, nothing to redo — never red (023 § 4).
5. **No button processes the queue. One bill, one click, with confirmation.** No app-side batch or serial queue: the agreement with the robot's side is to grow the batch slowly, and a queue surviving the app would write to the ERP unsupervised at the next launch (023 § 2; inherited 016).
6. **One gesture at a time; the second is refused**, here and by the robot's single-flight lock, which every Fantasma gesture shares (012).
7. **Sign of life before the call.** A run answers only at its end, minutes later; the busy mark goes on before the command leaves (AGENTS.md "The machine").
8. **"Encerrada" is the point of no return.** The Everest does not cancel a closed conferência. Past it, "Processar" and "Retirar" disappear (mid-run too), the note names the path, and the withdrawal is also refused where the file is handled (023 § 2).
9. **Resume, never redo.** A bill stopped between "Encerrada" and "Pedido criado" is not redone — redoing is the definition of a duplicated invoice; the screen says "retomar o pedido é o caminho" (inherited 016). The resume gesture itself is deferred (023 § 5).
10. **The reason is a key worded by the app; the robot's sentence rides beside it, verbatim, never interpreted** — a sentence improved on the other side must not break this screen in silence (023 § 4; inherited 016).
11. **A lot phrase is read by how it starts, never by what it contains.** "PROVÁVEL AGUARDANDO — …" contains "AGUARDANDO" and is still a question, not "aguardando"; an unrecognized phrase is a question, never "faturada" (023 § 7).
12. **Phases are progress, not state.** A lost phase costs a lagging bar, never a mistake; the bar only moves forward; the outcome at the end corrects it (023 § 3).
13. **"Stopped mid-run" means it died without an answer.** A run that ended in a refusal answered — it shows "Última falha", not the mid-run note (023 § 8).
14. **A confirmation never survives a selection change.** An open "Processar?" following the click onto another row would be one yes away from the wrong bill (023 § 8).
15. **Signing records a word and checks nothing** — for bills the robot's own list already closed (work from before this app) or settled by hand in the Everest (023 § 8).
16. **"Entregar" writes nothing to the ERP and needs no confirmation**; approval tries it on the way and may fail without failing the approval (023 § 2).
17. **"Retirar" order: the robot's verb first, our file after, the record last** — their refusal of anything past pending is the safety this side must not re-implement (023 § 2).
18. **The robot's cycle ends at "Pedido criado".** No "Faturada" step: a fifth step that never completes left every done bill looking unfinished (006 § 2).
19. **The first stage is called "No Faturamento".** "Fila" once meant two things (the dock's "aguarda extração" and this queue), and the operator went looking here for bills never approved; a second meaning for a glossary word is a defect (002; project memory 17/08/2026).
20. **The banner shows the robot's real session**, polled, never a remembered flag.
21. **The billing record stays on this machine.** It carries a patient's name, lives in the app's config directory, and never enters git or any export (023 § 1).

## Must survive the redesign

- Verdict and stage as two separate, simultaneously visible facts per row.
- No pill (no color, no placeholder word) for "not checked"; green only for "sem registro" earned by a run.
- The finished row's document number; Rule 1 endings never drawn as errors.
- The four stages with these words, ending at "Pedido criado"; the live movement during a run; the bar never moving backward.
- Gestures only on the one selected bill, one at a time, disabled while any is in flight; no bulk selection and no "process all".
- The inline confirmation texts for Processar, Retirar and Assinar, attached to the selected bill and closed by any selection change.
- "Processar" and "Retirar" absent (not just disabled) from "Encerrada" on, with the point-of-no-return sentence present.
- The mid-run note with "Recuperar o desfecho"; the four recovery outcomes worded apart.
- The abort reason worded + the robot's sentence verbatim in a code-like face; "Última falha" verbatim; lot phrases verbatim.
- The "Entregar" footnote (nothing written to the ERP) and the "Entregue ao Fantasma há …" fact.
- The robot banner reflecting the real session.
- Amber only for investigation (a human must decide) and the robot not ready; red for failures; accent for "workable now".

## Free to redesign

- The two-pane layout, the panel width, list-as-cards vs. table (this list does not use the shared Grid), row density.
- The segment bar's form (steps, dots, a timeline), as long as it reads as four ordered stages and stays apart from the verdict.
- Pill shapes and the edge-bar signal for workable/amber/red rows.
- Where the counters, the client filter and the view switch sit; the banner's form.
- The checklist's look; how the lots block is laid out; the order of the notes.
- Whether confirmations stay inline or become an anchored popover — provided they stay tied to the selected bill.

## Design opportunities

1. **Name the gesture in flight.** The banner says "Processando agora: …" for every gesture, including a delivery, a withdrawal, a recovery or a signature. "Entregando…", "Retirando…", "Recuperando o desfecho…" would say what is actually happening.
2. **Show why "Processar" cannot work before the confirmation.** The button stays enabled when the robot is off, without a session, or busy with a catalog collection or a query; the refusal arrives only after "Processar". Carrying the robot state (and its busy lock) onto the button, with the reason, would stop a confirmed click from going nowhere.
3. **An empty sentence per view.** "Nada em andamento" also answers "Concluídas" and "Todas" (and a client with nothing); each view deserves its own absence.
4. **The panel with nothing to show.** With an empty list the panel still asks to "Escolha uma conta na lista…".
5. **Deep-link a bill.** The Dashboard, the Revisão pointer and the search all land on the list with its top row selected, not on the bill they named.
6. **Client names, not ids.** The menu and the rows show raw adapter ids ("vale_verde"); the adapters carry display names.
7. **Counters as filters.** The four counters ignore the filters and cannot be clicked; making them the filters (a processar, provável, falha, concluída) — or saying they count every client — would remove a mismatch between the line and the list under it.
8. **Show the numbers where they matter.** An unfinished bill that closed conferência 170001 never shows the number (only finished rows do); the point-of-no-return note is where the operator needs it to resume the order in the Everest.
9. **Doors to the trail.** The "no outcome" recovery says "veja o rastro completo em Logs" without a door; failed runs could link to Logs · Fantasma · Sessão.
10. **A place for "Retomar o pedido".** The resume gesture is deferred (023 § 5); the design can reserve its spot beside the point-of-no-return note so the day it arrives nothing moves.
11. **A decision gesture for "provável".** The investigation note asks the operator to check the ERP "antes de decidir", but the only gesture offered is "Retirar"; "Assinar como resolvida" appears only after a recorded refusal.
12. **Singular item count.** "1 itens".
13. **One clear guidance for stopped runs.** The Dashboard says "recupere o desfecho antes de qualquer novo gesto", while this panel offers "Processar" and "Retirar" beside "Recuperar" when the stop happened below "Encerrada".
